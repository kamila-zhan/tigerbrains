Facing difficult emotions, struggling with procrastination or dealing any other problem related to mental health usually comes with not understanding where to start solving it.

Simple and clear instruction on what exactly to do is what needed.  
Mental problems suffer from having too many instructions.

There are tons of diverse psychotherapies, medications, advices from influencers, couches, philosophers and brain hacking methods.

To not be behind here is another instruction with 3-step flow to approach a mental problem.

1. Mindfulness
2. Automatic thoughts
3. Actions

Just like any other method, it does not guarantee nor immediate nor delayed help, but it for sure worth trying.

## 1. Mindfulness

Half of problem solution is to understand it.  
Mindfulness is a great way to identify everything relatable and possibly helpful in dealing with mental struggle.

Difference between simple thinking and being mindful is not being judgmental and not jumping to solutions or conclusions.  

Another aspect is to be mindful about current time only, without taking into account the past or the future. Current feelings, current situation, current needs. 

Basically this step is about gathering information with no analysis and actions yet. And it is important to not fall into analysis and emotions to keep data useful. 

Clean unbiased data on physical state, mental state, thoughts, emotions and happening situation or just reality, if it is not a particular event, is the desired result.

Important to note that being mindful is not an easy thing to do, but takes time to learn and practice to get better and get advantage of it.

[Site about mindfulness](https://www.mindful.org/what-is-mindfulness/)  
[Site about DBT, which includes mindfulness skill](https://dialecticalbehaviortherapy.com/mindfulness/)

## 2. Automatic thoughts

Based on cognitive behavioral therapy, thoughts play important role in mental issues.  
And good thing is working with them is measurable and more straightforward than working with emotions, for example.

Thoughts gathered in the first step each go through strict analysis and are being tested on whether they misrepresent reality and cause negative feelings.

Automatic negative thought states `everything is a disaster`.  
Mindfulness identifies this thought and that reality is a bit different.  
Finally through changing the thought to more rational one, like `everything is not that bad`, negative feelings should change too.
  
It is better to start with major cognitive distortions, which represent most of the negative thougts.  
[Online book with information about cognitive distortions](https://cogbtherapy.com/cbt-for-cognitive-distortions)

Tackling negative thought once will not help permanently.  
Usually negative thinking is a habit which needs to be tackled deliberately again and again.  

But each time it will be easier to turn thoughts into rational ones.  
Practice and consistency is the key to improvement.  

And for sure less negative and more rational thoughts will be and advantage while dealing with mental issue.

## 3. Actions

This step is about the most visible aspect of changing.  
Just do it part, which is loved by many people as the only part worth attention, which is not true.

First two steps are crucial before getting into actions.  
Without them it is hard to accurately identify what the action should be and how to make it more natural, not *just do it*.

The second step involved treating same negative thoughts as habits and tackling them over and over again.  
And here as continuation some behavior with negative impact is also being tackled over and over again, and it will be getting easier and the habit changes.  

While it is hard to identify how those habits or actions should look like overall.  
And sometimes it is indeed satisfactory to work only on mindfulness and thoughts.  

Still real change comes with actions, and its important to understand how the goal is structured with concrete actions and habits. 

For example, not caring about yourself while in bad mood may include not eating well. So the desired outcome is to eat well in the end of this 3-step flow.  

Skipping the first two steps and going straight to just eating will not affect the root of the problem and possibly cause even more struggle and inner conflict. 

For these reasons it is important to make all 3 steps and challenge the problem with being fully aware on the problem itself, change thinking and eventually change behavior.

## How to use

It is overwhelming to apply this flow.  
Best thing is to not proceed with the next step until you are comfortable with applying the previous one.  

It takes time to learn being mindful, changing automatic thoughts and changing habits.  
But still the idea is that until all the steps are not done it is hard to achieve satisfactory results.  

Start with mindfulness and it is alone should already help to some point.  
Once comfortable move on to learning thoughts changing techniques. And so on.
