Facing difficult emotions, struggling with procrastination or dealing any other problem related to mental health usually comes with not understanding where to start solving it.

Simple and clear instruction on what exactly to do is what needed.  
Mental problems suffer from having too many instructions.

There are tons of diverse psychotherapies, medications, advices from influencers, couches, philosophers and brain hacking methods.

To not be behind here is another instruction with 3-step flow to approach a mental problem.

1. Mindfulness
2. Thoughts
3. Actions / Habits

Just like any other method, it does not guarantee nor immediate nor delayed help, but it for sure worth trying.
