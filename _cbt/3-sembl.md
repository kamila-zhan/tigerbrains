There are things which require attention every day or almost every day.

1. Sleep
2. Eat
3. Move
4. Breathe
5. Learn

They form the basis of well-being.

To remember all five, use `SEMBL` abbreviation.

As a practice of mindfulness throughout the day ask yourself:
- Did I get enough sleep?
- Did I eat properly? Am I hungry?
- When was the last time I moved my body? Walking, simple stretching, any body movement counts.
- Am I calm? Do I breathe slowly?
- Did I exercise my brain today? Anything new counts: new music, new food, new knowledge etc.

### 1. Sleep

Lack of sleep is strongly connected with mental health issues.  
Anxiety, depression, mood changes are all worsened without proper sleeping.

Specifically, poor sleep impairs the prefrontal cortex and hyperactivates the amygdala.

Impaired prefrontal cortex means worsened impulse control and decision-making.

Hyperactivated amygdala means more fear, stress and the prefrontal cortex is not here to help.

There are a lot more sleep-brain facts, but the mentioned ones are enough to see that better sleeping alone will essentially help with mental problems and to see why lack of sleep may cause helplessness in front of negative feelings.

*[How Poor Sleep Affects Your Brain and Mental Health](https://neurosity.co/guides/poor-sleep-brain-mental-health)*

### 2. Eat

The digestive system has a direct connection with the nervous system.  

It is hard to give general advice, as food is too personal.  
Everyone has struggles, preferences and specific physical conditions.

But at least try to eat on time.  
Eat balanced meals and stay hydrated.

*[Food and mental health](https://www.mind.org.uk/information-support/tips-for-everyday-living/food-and-mental-health/)*

### 3. Move

The aim is not to do a full workout.  

Simply any movement is already enough.  

*[Exercise and mental health](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/healthyliving/exercise-and-mental-health)*

### 4. Breathe

Just breathe. Slowly.  

Repeat as much as you can during the day.

*[Breathing to reduce stress](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/healthyliving/breathing-to-reduce-stress)*

### 5. Learn

Learning is like a gym for the brain.  

A stronger brain is more power to deal with mental issues.  

Learning is any new thing, it should not necessarily be academically hard.  

*[learning new skills for your mental health](https://changemh.org/resources/skills-development-and-mental-health/)*

### Small steps

No doubt managing to have good sleep, good food, exercising both brain and body and breathing can feel like an impossible task.  

The point is to identify what can be improved with the least effort right now and try.  

After one of `SEMBL` becomes better, more energy and brain power will be produced to continue enhancements.  

Small steps and consistency do change the brain for the better.  

Make a small step.
