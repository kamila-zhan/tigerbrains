When someone asks us to talk about ourselves, we usually get stuck and don't know what to say.

The idea that we should spend time on learning something about our own selves sounds strange.  
But we should.

It is also disappointing to realise that we do not choose much.  
We may only affect and encourage ourselves into something we prefer or force.  
And it is the same as talking to another person, hopefully a friend, not an enemy.  

Do I like that pepperoni pizza?  
I will find that out about myself when I try, but I can't decide.  
The most I can do is persuade myself that it's tasty.  
Luckily, I love pepperoni pizza.

While knowing whether I like pizza is not very useful, it is beneficial to know:
- What are the conditions for me to work efficiently?
- How to help me cope with stress?
- What makes me angry?
- How to ease the process of physical workouts?
- How to keep me engaged while I study?
- What activities do I like the most?

The list of questions depends on your priorities, aspirations in life and any other demands and desires.  

Our conscious mind is Dora the Explorer with all the other parts inside.  

The more we know about ourselves, the more powerful we are.  
We are our own tool, and it is an amazing tool if we master it.

### Methods of self-discovery

It takes time.  

Ask questions.  
Conduct full interviews. 

Experiment.  
Like trying the pizza, try various methods, approaches and actions if you are trying to find the best way to perform.  
Try different activities and variations if trying to find out what interests you.

Practice mindfulness.  
It is a powerful method to start noticing your reactions (thoughts, emotions) in response to different events.  

Talk to yourself in a manner that makes you feel welcomed to open up.  
Being harsh is not inviting.  
The inner you can talk only in a safe environment.

Listen.

What do you feel towards yourself?  
It is okay if the feelings are not good.  
Just remember that warm and accepting feelings are crucial for practicing self-discovery.
