Making and maintaining progress requires energy, and sometimes you will run out of that energy.  

### Work with triggers

Identifying the triggers and removing them as much as possible is one way to preserve your battery.

This is hard to do if the trigger is a big part of your life, like your job.  

But some small changes can still be made, like avoiding certain events or spending less time on what bothers you.

Your outside environment is part of your mental health.  
Improve it too along with personal growth.

### Believe

Fear of change is ingrained in us because everything new, including new thoughts, emotions or behavior, is a potential threat.

You may remember that fear is driven by the amygdala in our brain.  
One way to calm it down is to **believe**.  
Conscious belief activates the prefrontal cortex, which shuts down the panic alarm and helps to battle anxiety.

Believing will also help to keep going while the progress is yet to come.  
Keep being mindful and remind yourself that small steps and consistency are the driving power for your engine.

*[Why Change Feels So Threatening](https://www.anxietytherapistchicago.com/fear-of-change-anxiety/)* 

### Prepare for setbacks

If you experience a setback, you may feel lost and lose hope.  

After all the work done, getting back to the same negative feelings, thoughts or behavior is a kick in the face.  

However, it is an honour.  
Setback is a privilege for those who have already learnt how to make progress.  
What helped you once to reach it will help you again.

David Burns said about setbacks: "The question is not whether it will happen, but when it will happen."

Therefore, prepare yourself by thinking about how you might feel and how you might support yourself beforehand.
 
*[Dealing With Setbacks During Therapy](https://www.therapyroute.com/article/dealing-with-setbacks-during-therapy-by-therapyroute)*

### Acknowledge your power

A lot was said about what and how you should change.  
However, the endless race for the better is exhausting.  

Find where you are already powerful and your current strengths. 

Pay attention to your strengths first before moving on to what needs improvement.
